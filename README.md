# Imagend

**Turn a plain idea into a model-ready image or video prompt.** Live at [imagendai.com](https://www.imagendai.com).

Imagend writes prompts tuned for each major AI image and video model, with a Lazy Guide for people who don't know where to start, brand-consistent styles, voice typing and a saved library.
