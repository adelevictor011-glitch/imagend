Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

The short version

  • Your ideas, prompts, library and history stay in your browser. Imagend's prompt engine runs on your device, so we never receive them.
  • We store only what we need to run your account: your Google name and email, your plan, how many prompts you used each day, and payment references.
  • We never see or store card or bank details. Flutterwave handles payments.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Made by Youni Ltd (RC 9411546), Lagos, Nigeria, is the data controller for Imagend. Contact us about anything in this policy at hello@imagendai.com.

This policy follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, it also explains your rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR.

3. What stays on your device

  • Your ideas, prompts, saved library, history and settings are kept in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • Share links contain your prompt setup inside the link itself. Anyone you send the link to can see that setup.
  • Voice typing uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Your browser's provider (for example Google in Chrome or Apple in Safari) processes the audio to turn it into text. Imagend never receives your audio, and nothing you say is added to a form until you tap “Use this text”.
  • We store a random device ID in local storage. It is only used, in coded form, for the free-trial check above.
  • We use local storage instead of advertising or tracking cookies. Sign-in uses local storage to keep you logged in.

5. International transfers

Some of our providers store or process data outside Nigeria, for example in the United States or Europe. When that happens we rely on the safeguards allowed by Sections 41 to 43 of the NDPA, such as the provider's contractual data-protection commitments. Where those are not available, we rely on the transfer being necessary to provide the service you asked for.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, plan and usage data: while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Payment records: 6 years, to meet Nigerian tax and accounting requirements, even after you delete your account.
  • Free-trial check records: 12 months after the trial starts, then deleted.
  • Technical logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period, usually a few days to a few weeks.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold your data and give you a copy
  • correct data that is wrong
  • delete your data (except records we must keep by law)
  • stop or restrict using your data, or object to how we use it
  • give you your data in a portable format
  • withdraw any consent you have given

Email hello@imagendai.com from the email on your account. We will reply within 30 days. There is no charge.

You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng). In the EU or UK, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

8. How we protect data

  • All connections use encryption (HTTPS).
  • Our database only lets each user read their own records; plans and usage can only be changed by our server.
  • Every payment is checked directly with Flutterwave before a plan is activated.
  • Access to admin tools is limited to the founder.

If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours and tell affected users without undue delay.

9. Children

Imagend is not designed for children. Users under 18 should only use it with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us data without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and tell you in the app or by email about significant changes.
