Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

The short version

Use Imagend to make things you have the right to make. Don't use it to deceive, harm, sexualise or impersonate people, break the law, or get around our limits.

1. Don't use Imagend to create prompts for

  • Any sexual or sexualised content involving minors. We report this to the authorities.
  • Sexual or intimate images of real people without their consent, or “deepfakes” that put real people in situations that never happened.
  • Impersonating real people, brands or organisations, or fake documents, IDs, receipts, certificates or official notices.
  • Scams, fraud, fake product claims or misleading adverts.
  • Copies of other people's copyrighted characters, artwork or logos, or counterfeit branding, without permission.
  • Content that promotes violence, terrorism, self-harm, or hatred against people because of who they are.
  • Harassment, bullying or threats against anyone.
  • Deceptive political content, such as fake images of candidates or officials meant to mislead voters.
  • Anything else that is illegal in Nigeria or where you live.

2. Don't misuse the service

  • Don't try to hack, overload, scrape or copy Imagend or its prompt engine.
  • Don't get around daily limits or payments, for example with multiple free accounts or a shared account.
  • Don't resell Imagend access or package its prompts as your own product without our written permission.
  • Don't use someone else's account or payment details.

3. Follow the rules of the AI tools you use

Midjourney, Flux, Ideogram, Recraft, Nano Banana, Veo, Kling, Runway, Luma, Seedance and other tools have their own content rules. You are responsible for following them when you use a prompt from Imagend.

4. What happens if these rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may warn you, limit your account, suspend or close it, and report illegal activity to the relevant authorities. If we close an account for a serious breach, we do not refund the remaining plan period.

5. Report a problem

If you see Imagend being used to harm someone, email hello@imagendai.com. We read every report.
